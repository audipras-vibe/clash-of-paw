PROJECT WHITEPAPER · VERSION 0.1

Clash of Paw

A game-first dog tower battler with fair competition at its core.

Read this first. This is a concept paper for an in-development game. It is not a token sale, investment offer, or promise of rewards.

1. A game first

Clash of Paw is a fast, dog-themed 1v1 tower battler. Each player protects a Kennel, gathers energy, deploys a chosen pack, and uses a limited tower skill to open a path through the opposing team. A match is won by bringing down the rival Kennel before time expires.

The core game must be fun with no wallet, purchase, or token. Competitive integrity is a product requirement, not a feature to sell.

2. The pack

Players build a squad from distinct dog roles. The first release centers on simple, readable units:

  • Scout: low cost, fast pressure.
  • Bulldog: durable front-line defender.
  • Hound: slower, high-impact attacker.

Future packs can add support, ranged, control, and siege roles. Every playable unit should be obtainable through normal play. Competitive modes use normalized levels and clearly published balance rules.

3. Modes

Quick Match is the low-friction AI mode used to learn units and test pack ideas.

PvP Arena is planned for ranked and friendly 1v1 play. Matchmaking, replays, anti-cheat, and a transparent seasonal rating system are prerequisites for ranked rewards.

The Market is planned as an optional destination for cosmetics and, only after appropriate technical and legal review, player-to-player ownership features. Cosmetic rarity never changes combat strength.

4. Progression and fair play

Progression unlocks cosmetic expression, collection goals, and new ways to play. It must not sell match wins. Balance changes are documented; ranked rules use fixed or normalized stats; and gameplay-affecting content is earnable without an on-chain asset.

5. Future Web3 approach

Web3 is an optional layer, not the game’s foundation. A player can use every essential gameplay feature without connecting a wallet. If digital collectibles are introduced, they will be cosmetic or access-oriented, have no promise of resale value, and be clearly separated from ranked competitive power.

No token, chain, wallet provider, collectible supply, sale date, or marketplace launch is committed by this paper.

6. Solana-ready, wallet-optional

Clash of Paw is intended to be built for the Solana ecosystem. The first integration is deliberately lightweight: an optional wallet connection that can serve as a portable player identity. The core game remains playable without a wallet.

  • A connected public address may identify a player’s profile, cosmetic loadout, achievements, and future match history.
  • Future PvP can use this identity for tournament registration and eligible reward claims.
  • The Market may later support optional player-to-player cosmetic ownership and trading, subject to technical, security, legal, and regional review.

Wallet connection will never determine match strength, gate core gameplay, or be required to enjoy Quick Match.

7. Proposed economy: $TREATS

Clash of Paw does not launch a tradeable token with the game. Instead, it uses an off-chain, non-transferable earned currency called $TREATS.

  • Earned from play, quests, and season objectives.
  • Spent on cosmetics, profile items, and non-competitive convenience features.
  • Never sold as an investment product, cash-redeemable, or required to enter a fair ranked match.
  • Sources, sinks, and inflation controls are published before any live economy update.

This avoids a second currency, speculative pressure, and the conflict between rewarding play and preserving a stable competitive game.

8. If a governance asset is ever considered

Any future governance asset requires a separate proposal, legal review in launch jurisdictions, security review, public disclosures, and a community consultation period. It should not be sold as an expected-return opportunity or used to gate core gameplay.

9. Revenue and sustainability

The project can be supported through optional cosmetics, battle-pass-style cosmetic tracks, and properly disclosed marketplace fees if the Market launches. Revenue pays for continued development, live operations, moderation, security, and community programs. It does not create a promise of player income.

10. Roadmap

  1. Improve the playable web demo and collect balance feedback.
  2. Add more original dog units, accessibility settings, and local progression.
  3. Build optional Solana identity, then PvP foundations: authoritative multiplayer, matchmaking, anti-cheat, replay review, and seasons.
  4. Consider optional cosmetic identity and market features only after the core game is stable and relevant reviews are complete.

11. Risks and player communication

Clash of Paw is a game project in active development. Features may change, be delayed, or be cancelled. Digital items can lose utility; services can change or end; third-party platforms and blockchain systems carry technical and regulatory risks. Nothing in this document is financial, legal, or investment advice.
