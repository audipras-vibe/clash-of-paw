Clash of Paw

A game-first dog tower battler with fair competition, expressive ownership, and an economy that earns the right to exist.

Read this first. This document describes a proposed product and economy. It is not a token sale, investment offer, promise of profit, financial advice, or legal conclusion. No tradeable token is live or committed.

1. A game worth playing first

Clash of Paw is a fast 1v1 lane battler. Each player protects a Kennel, generates energy, summons a three-role pack, and times team abilities to break the opposing formation. A match lasts three minutes. Energy generation doubles in the final minute, producing a deliberate late-game surge.

The current vertical slice is playable without an account, wallet, purchase, collectible, or token. That remains a permanent product rule.

2. The founding pack

  • Brutus · Guardian: durable frontline fighter who shields nearby allies.
  • Sage · Healer: support specialist who restores the most wounded pack member.
  • Hex · Mage: fragile ranged attacker whose Paw-Fire damages clustered enemies.

Every combat role has a free gameplay version. Future breeds and classes expand strategy through balanced sidegrades—not progressively stronger paid units. Ranked play uses normalized stats and published balance changes.

3. Modes and competitive integrity

Quick Match provides Easy, Normal, and Hard AI. Friendly PvP is the first online milestone. Ranked Arena follows only after authoritative servers, replay review, anti-cheat, disconnect handling, and seasonal matchmaking are proven.

Entry to standard ranked play cannot require a wallet, NFT, paid pass, or tradeable token. Tournament formats involving prizes require separate rules, age and regional controls, and legal review.

4. Character collectibles without pay-to-win

Brutus, Sage, Hex, and future heroes may have limited-edition collectible identities. Ownership can unlock the associated visual skin, portrait treatment, profile badge, lore card, emote, and exhibition rights defined in the item license. It does not increase HP, damage, energy generation, matchmaking priority, or reward rate.

The Barracks is the recruitment surface. Players spend account-bound Treats earned through play on clearly disclosed recruitment contracts for standard hero and cosmetic variants. Duplicate protection, deterministic progress, and published odds are required before any randomized recruitment is enabled. A non-owner can always play the same combat role with the same normalized stats.

Optional PAW collector editions may be offered only after the ecosystem launch gates pass. A free or Treats-based route must remain available, and collectible metadata, media hosting, creator royalties, custody support, and commercial-use rights must be disclosed before minting.

5. Two-layer economy

Treats · the game currency

Treats are off-chain, account-bound, non-transferable points. Players earn them from matches, tutorials, quests, achievements, and season objectives. They are spent on Barracks recruitment, standard cosmetics, emotes, profile customization, and crafting. Treats have no cash redemption, exchange listing, governance weight, or promised monetary value. Treats are separate from the regenerating battle energy used to deploy heroes during a match.

PAW · a conditional ecosystem token

PAW is a proposed fixed-supply Solana ecosystem token, not a launch requirement. If approved through the gates below, it may support cosmetic marketplace settlement, creator publishing deposits, community curation, and limited governance over community programs. It cannot buy combat stats, ranked access, favorable matchmaking, or guaranteed rewards.

6. Utility and sinks

  • Barracks recruitment: Treats are retired when a player recruits standard hero or cosmetic variants; recruitment cannot create stronger competitive stats.
  • Cosmetic crafting: optional PAW payment for scarce visual recipes; a published share is permanently retired or routed to the community treasury.
  • Creator publishing: refundable anti-spam deposits for submitted cosmetic collections, returned when review milestones are met.
  • Marketplace settlement: optional settlement asset alongside supported non-token payment routes where available.
  • Community curation: time-locked PAW signals preferences for themes, creator grants, and community event budgets.
  • Collector access: cosmetic presales or exhibition events, never exclusive combat power.

There is no passive staking yield, guaranteed buyback, revenue share, dividend, price support, or play-to-earn emission loop. Rewards, if any, are budgeted promotions with published caps and eligibility—not an expectation of income.

8. Treasury and governance

Before any PAW launch, treasury control requires a public signer policy, independent signers, transaction delay, spending limits, incident recovery process, conflicts register, and quarterly asset/liability report. Governance begins with low-risk scopes such as community themes and grant recommendations. Protocol upgrades, treasury transfers, and emergency powers require higher quorum, longer voting periods, and security review.

Votes do not create ownership of the company, a claim on revenue, fiduciary rights, or a promise that proposals will increase token value.

9. Sustainable business model

The studio can earn revenue from cosmetic sales, optional seasonal cosmetic passes, creator tools, brand collaborations, and a clearly disclosed marketplace service fee. Suggested marketplace fee design: 2.5% platform fee, with creator royalties displayed separately and enforced only where technically and legally supportable.

Studio revenue funds development, servers, support, moderation, security, art, esports operations, and reserves. Token holders receive no revenue distribution.

10. Token launch gates

  1. Product: stable public game, measurable retention, working economy sinks, and no dependence on token funding to finish the core loop.
  2. Legal: written analysis for every target jurisdiction, sanctions and AML assessment, age controls, tax treatment, marketing review, and required registrations or licensed partners.
  3. Security: audited contracts, verified vesting, multisig, timelock, incident response, bug bounty, and public mint/freeze authority policy.
  4. Market integrity: published allocations and wallets, insider trading/conflicts policy, market-maker terms, liquidity risk disclosure, and no price promises.
  5. Community: public consultation followed by a documented go/no-go decision. The valid outcome may be “do not launch.”

Indonesia’s crypto-asset trading oversight sits with OJK, whose framework has continued to change, including POJK 23/2025 and implementing rules effective September 2026. In the United States, the treatment of crypto-related transactions depends on their facts, promises, and distribution structure; the SEC published updated interpretive guidance in 2026. These references are checkpoints for counsel, not conclusions about PAW.

11. Roadmap

  1. Playable foundation: deepen AI combat, accessibility, feedback, balance telemetry, and device performance.
  2. Collection without chain dependency: accounts, inventory, cosmetic loadouts, progression, seasons, and creator pipeline.
  3. Online competition: authoritative friendly PvP, replays, moderation, then ranked seasons.
  4. Optional ownership: wallet identity and cosmetic collectibles after license, custody, metadata, security, and regional reviews.
  5. Economy decision: validate Treats sources and sinks; publish PAW consultation and launch-gate evidence; launch, defer, or cancel.

12. Risks and change policy

Game features, collectibles, allocations, utilities, chains, and timelines may change or be cancelled. Digital assets can lose all market value or utility. Smart contracts, wallets, bridges, marketplaces, regulation, taxation, and third-party infrastructure introduce material risks. The team will version this document, retain prior versions, and publish material changes with reasons.
