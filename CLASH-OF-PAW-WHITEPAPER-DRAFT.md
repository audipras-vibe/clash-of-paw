# Clash of Paw — Game and Economy Design Paper

**Version:** 0.2 · September 2026  
**Status:** Product-design draft. Not a token sale, investment offer, promise of profit, financial advice, or legal conclusion.

## Product thesis

Clash of Paw is a three-minute 1v1 dog tower battler. Players protect a Kennel, generate energy, summon a three-role pack, and time team abilities. Energy generation doubles during the final minute.

The game remains playable without an account, wallet, purchase, collectible, or token. Ranked play uses normalized stats.

## Founding pack

- **Brutus · Guardian:** durable frontline fighter who shields nearby allies.
- **Sage · Healer:** restores the most wounded pack member.
- **Hex · Mage:** ranged attacker whose Paw-Fire damages clustered enemies.

Every combat role has a free version with identical competitive stats. Collectible editions may change art, animation, profile presentation, lore, and emotes, but never HP, damage, energy, matchmaking, or reward rate.

## Modes

1. Quick Match against Easy, Normal, or Hard AI.
2. Authoritative Friendly PvP with replay and disconnect handling.
3. Ranked Arena only after anti-cheat, moderation, and seasonal matchmaking are proven.

Standard ranked entry never requires a wallet, NFT, paid pass, or tradeable token.

## Two-layer economy

### Treats

Treats are off-chain, account-bound, non-transferable points earned through play and spent on Barracks recruitment, ordinary cosmetics, emotes, profiles, and crafting. They have no cash redemption, exchange listing, governance weight, or promised monetary value. Treats are separate from the regenerating battle energy used to deploy heroes during a match.

### The Barracks

The Barracks is the recruitment surface for standard hero and cosmetic variants. Recruitment spends Treats and must provide duplicate protection, deterministic progress, and published odds before any randomized mechanic is enabled. Every combat role retains a free version with normalized stats. Optional PAW collector editions require the ecosystem launch gates and cannot buy combat power.

### PAW — conditional, not committed

PAW is a proposed fixed-supply Solana ecosystem token. It may support optional cosmetic marketplace settlement, creator publishing deposits, community curation, and limited governance over community programs. It cannot buy competitive power, ranked access, favorable matchmaking, or guaranteed rewards.

- **Maximum supply:** 1,000,000,000 PAW.
- **Additional minting:** disabled by revoking mint authority after verified genesis.
- **Protocol-promised yield:** none.
- **Required for core gameplay:** never.
- **Genesis circulating-supply target:** no more than 12%.

There is no passive staking yield, guaranteed buyback, revenue share, dividend, price support, or play-to-earn emission loop.

## Provisional PAW allocation

| Bucket | Share | Release discipline |
|---|---:|---|
| Community and player ecosystem | 35% | Programmatic release over at least 60 months with campaign caps |
| Community treasury | 20% | Multisig, timelock, quarterly reporting, decentralization milestones |
| Team and future contributors | 18% | 12-month cliff, then monthly vesting over 36 months |
| Ecosystem and creator partners | 12% | Milestone-based grants over at least 36 months |
| Market liquidity | 8% | Published market-making mandate, wallets, limits, and reports |
| Potential community distribution | 7% | Separate jurisdictional review, terms, eligibility, lockups, and risks |

## Sustainable revenue

Studio revenue may come from cosmetic sales, optional cosmetic season passes, creator tools, collaborations, and a disclosed marketplace service fee. The current fee design target is 2.5%, with creator royalties shown separately. Revenue funds operations and is not distributed to token holders.

## Launch gates

PAW may proceed only after all five gates pass:

1. **Product:** stable live game, measurable retention, tested sources and sinks.
2. **Legal:** written jurisdiction analysis, AML/sanctions review, age controls, tax and marketing review, and licensed partners or registrations where required.
3. **Security:** audited contracts, verified vesting, multisig, timelock, incident response, and bug bounty.
4. **Market integrity:** public allocations and wallets, conflicts policy, market-maker terms, and no price promises.
5. **Community:** public consultation and documented go/no-go decision. “Do not launch” remains valid.

## Roadmap

1. Polish the playable foundation, accessibility, balance, and performance.
2. Add accounts, inventory, progression, seasons, and creator pipeline without chain dependency.
3. Build authoritative friendly PvP, replay review, moderation, then ranked play.
4. Add optional wallet identity and cosmetic collectibles after license, custody, metadata, security, and regional reviews.
5. Validate the Treats economy, publish PAW launch-gate evidence, then launch, defer, or cancel.

## Risks and change policy

Features, assets, allocations, utilities, chains, and timelines may change or be cancelled. Digital assets can lose all market value or utility. Smart contracts, wallets, marketplaces, regulation, taxation, and third-party services introduce material risk. Material revisions must be versioned and explained publicly.
